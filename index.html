Why we exist

We don't sell the car. We sell the evening.

The yellow cab is honest, the rideshare is convenient, the black car app is fast. None of them know that the curtain rises at 8:00 sharp, that the Bernard B. Jacobs is on 45th and not 47th, that Carbone holds the back booth for someone who asked at 4 PM.

We are based in Yonkers, ten miles north of Midtown. Our chauffeurs drive this corridor at three in the morning without a GPS. They know which side of Lexington the doorman watches, which restaurant prefers the side-door pull-up, which terminal at JFK runs forty minutes ahead of every other.

Reserve a chauffeur, and the evening runs itself — bespoke, unhurried, on schedule.
